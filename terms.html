Booking Terms & Cancellation

What to expect when you book a stay at Diva Patong Hotel.

Check-in & check-out

Specific check-in and check-out times are not yet published on this site. Please confirm them with the hotel directly by phone or email before you travel. Contact details are below.

Cancellation policy

A specific cancellation window and any associated fees are not yet published on this site. Please confirm the cancellation policy for your dates directly with the hotel before booking, and get it in writing (email is fine) if the cancellation terms matter to your plans.

Payment

This website does not collect payment online. Accepted payment methods at the property (cash, card, or otherwise) should be confirmed with the hotel directly.

Rates shown on this site

Room rates on this site are per night and are what the hotel has provided; two room types are currently marked "Ask for rates" because a confirmed nightly rate hasn't been supplied yet. All rates are subject to change and to confirmation at the time of booking.

Questions

For anything not covered here, contact the hotel directly: reservation@diva-patong-hotel.com or +66 (0) 65 740 1917.
