Privacy Policy

How this website handles the information it collects.

This page reflects what the site currently does, and will be revised as new features (such as an online booking form) are added.

What this site currently collects

At present this website does not have a live booking form, guest-details form, or any other data-entry feature. It is an informational site with links out to the hotel's existing external booking system. Browsing this site does not currently submit any personal information to us.

Once an on-site booking request form is added, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what it collects (typically name, email, phone number, and stay details), why, and how long it is kept.

Payment information

This website does not collect card numbers or other payment details online. Any payment for a stay is arranged directly with the hotel.

Cookies and similar technology

This site does not currently set analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If that changes (for example, to add site analytics or an embedded map service), this page will list exactly which services are used and what they collect before they go live.

Third-party services

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The Contact & Location page and the homepage embed an interactive map from OpenStreetMap, which may set its own cookies or log your IP address when the map loads. This is standard for any embedded map and is controlled by OpenStreetMap, not by us. A "Get directions" link on the same pages opens Google Maps in a new tab. Both services are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one: OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Contacting us
