Comfort at the centre of Patong

Diva Patong Hotel sits on Nanai Road, close enough to Bangla Road to walk, close enough to the beach to hear it. An open-air pool, an in-house spa, and a kitchen running Thai, Indian, Italian and vegan menus side by side mean you rarely need to leave the building. Until you want to.

Ten room types, from a simple double to a rooftop penthouse with its own bar and jacuzzi. Pick the one that matches how loud you want the trip to be.

Pool, spa, restaurant & Stella Bar

The four reasons guests say they didn't need to leave for a whole afternoon.

The Pool

Open-air, ringed by loungers that fill up by mid-morning. Towels at the desk, no extra charge.

The Spa

Traditional Thai massage and treatments on-site, no taxi to a wellness centre required.

The Restaurant

Thai, Indian, Italian and vegan menus side by side, served in the dining room beside Stella Bar.

Stella Bar

Pool tables, cold drinks, and a crowd that doesn't thin out until late. Attached to the restaurant, open to the street.

Worth leaving the pool for

Eight places within reach of the hotel, from the beach out front to the hill above the island.

Patong Beach

Steps from the hotel

The beach the whole street is named for. Sand and sun loungers, then the walk back up for a shower before dinner.

Bangla Road

A short walk, after dark

Neon, noise, and the reason some guests ask for a room facing away from it. We can point you to it either way.

Jungceylon

Shopping, air-conditioned

Patong's shopping centre: cinema, food court, and a break from the heat when the beach gets old.

Banzaan Market

Where the kitchens shop

Fresh seafood, fruit, and produce stalls a short walk from Bangla Road. Best early, before the heat sets in.

Freedom & Karon Beach

A short taxi south

Two quieter coves for the day you want a beach without Patong's noise.

Old Phuket Town

Worth the taxi

Sino-Portuguese shopfronts, coffee, and a slower pace than the coast.

Wat Chalong

Phuket's most-visited temple

Ornate roofs and a working monastery, south of Patong.

Big Buddha

The view from the hilltop

Forty-five metres of white marble, with the best panorama on the island.
