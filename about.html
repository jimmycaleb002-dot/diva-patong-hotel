About Diva Patong Hotel

On Nanai Road, close enough to Patong Beach and Bangla Road to walk to either.

Where we are

Diva Patong Hotel sits on Nanai Road in Patong, Phuket, close enough to Bangla Road to walk, close enough to the beach to hear it. It's a location built around not needing a taxi for the basics: the sand, the nightlife, and the shops of Jungceylon are all within reasonable walking distance.

What's on site

Ten room types run from a simple double to a rooftop penthouse with its own private bar and jacuzzi, so the room can match the trip rather than the other way around. An open-air pool sits at the centre of the property, ringed by loungers; an in-house spa offers traditional Thai massage and treatments without a taxi to a wellness centre. Stella Bar (pool tables, cold drinks, attached to the restaurant and open to the street) keeps going after the kitchen closes.

The kitchen itself runs Thai, Indian, Italian and vegan menus side by side, which is less a gimmick and more a reflection of who actually stays here.
